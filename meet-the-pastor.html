Meet the Pastor

Larry started his working life as a storeman in Richmond, then shipping clerk, fireman and Youth Corrections Worker. He also trained in Judo, and became the Australian Heavyweight Champion!

A great change came to his life when God took control of him. He turned his life over to Jesus Christ! He and Sharon were married, and they have been involved in continuous ministry together since 1975 having met in the Salvation Army Training College, Larry in 1973 and Sharon in 1972.

Their Salvation Army service continued until 1995. During this time, they were in charge of Corps in: Benalla, Swan Hill, Wodonga and Campbelltown SA.

A 4-year term in Argentina was followed by a Corps in Ballarat. In 1989 their ministry changed to Homeless Men’s work, followed by Prison ministry, concluding with time at The Bridge Centre for drug rehab in St Kilda. Three lovely daughters were born during their early ministry years.

God's Holy Spirit led them into The Churches of Christ in 1995, where they served 25 years, pastoring churches in Swan Hill, Springvale South and Dandenong. Another turn of God's leading saw Larry taking up interim ministry within the Baptist Church at Millgrove during the time of Covid lockdown, concluding this term in December 2022.

Pastor Larry then became part of the friendly Wandin community, and he is always seeking new ways of communicating God's message and love.

Ministries

  • Ladies' bible study

    Ladies meet on the second and fourth Mondays of the month, at 10am. It is a time to dig into the Bible and unpack what it means for us today. Ladies also welcome support in prayer and encourage each other, growing together as a community of women serving a great God.

  • Men’s bible study

    Men normally meet on Tuesday evenings for Bible study at 7 pm, but we don't meet on the first Tuesday of the month. and we don't meet during June, July or August while we are looking after homeless people in the church. Come and join us when we are meeting!

  • Prayer Chain

    If you have a prayer need, please let us know and we will discreetly pray with and/or for you.

  • Craft Group

    Ladies craft group meets at 1 pm on the second and fourth Mondays after a BYO lunch. Ladies enjoy the sharing of ideas and talents, and the opportunity to learn new skills. Lots of talking, laughing and afternoon tea is associated with this group.

  • Wandin Players

    The Wandin Players Drama Group was established in 2007 and has performed productions of comedies each year. They rehearse in the church on Wednesday Evenings. New members are always welcome.
